# Exact-span repair and semantic-review routing

Six normal model calls: four previously identified failures and two factual clean controls. All six proposals were valid exact-span edits; all protected markers restored. All four targeted defects were repaired under primary-session review, and both controls remained byte-identical. One of four repaired cases passed fidelity, cadence and mode jointly. The other three remained cadence-limited. This is assisted development evidence: the primary session supplied defect spans and reasons. It is not an independent critic study or author approval.

Production replay at d4a279004 retained three factual repairs in the revision API but no channel output. A negation removed during a correct repair caused an unconditional fidelity failure; equivalent historical qualification and contraction changes also prevented final semantic review. The changed implementation routes these lexical signals to explicit review while preserving hard quantity, command, citation and protected-content failures. With the same frozen outputs and judgments, it retained all four factual repairs in the revision API, and the channel retained the single joint-pass conversation. Six of seven destination replays still fell back; no-callback behavior stayed unchanged in every check. No additional model calls were made for this comparison.

Generation cost: 9,869 tokens; service-cycle wall time 79.6s, including setup and restoration. Critique and final review were primary-session assisted and their model cost was not measured. Revision replay timing is local replay time, not online pipeline latency; its reserved callback tokens are accounting bounds, not additional measured usage. Fortemi was restored healthy and the test model unloaded.

The remaining limitation is substantial: exact-span repair preserves outside bytes but can remove some of the apparent cadence when that cadence depended on an unsupported causal explanation. It does not create voice where the draft lacks it. Next evaluate independent defect localization and a second model family, keeping author/reader qualification separate.
