There were three factual gains and six losses. The added guidance did not establish a cadence improvement. Source inventories and generic headings persisted; new defects included unsupported comparisons, assigned reader implementation work, an emotional organizational stance, and a malformed evidential relationship. All 24 destination replays retained originals under the joint gate, and no-callback originals were preserved.

Generation usage increased 31.6%, excluding primary review and warmup. The service cycle took 156.0s including setup/restoration. Fortemi was restored healthy and the model unloaded. Same pinned GPT-OSS20b digest, seed 2303, temperature 0.3, medium reasoning and 4096 output ceiling as the baseline. JDS guidance had 737 characters and Darwin 3044; this is not an equal-budget author comparison. No replacement draws, repairs or private-profile edits occurred.

This is primary-session development evidence; the judge previously created profiles and designed the tests. Omission of companion guidance was real, but supplying it is not sufficient to solve quality. Do not promote this adapter addition or claim that broader human qualification is complete.

A sparse/rich-notes comparison across all four development voices is prepared but has not run. Keep topic, model, guidance condition and rendering separate, preserve all short-brief failures, and do not lower the existing cadence criterion.
