# Civic safety

**Enforcement: BLOCK**

Fail closed when jurisdiction, consent, authorization, terms, license, meeting
openness, or private/public status is unresolved. Never bypass authentication,
paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, IP blocks, encryption, private URLs, or robots
denials. Retrieval permission and publication permission are separate.

Non-overridable blocked classes are covert/private recording; credential or
access-control bypass; doxxing; stalking; biometric deanonymization; targeting
homes, families, or routines; threats; intimidation; impersonation; coordinated
contact floods; and inferring redacted, sealed, confidential-source, or closed
session content.

Offer safe alternatives: official recordings/APIs/exports, a lawfully supplied
copy, an official office/contact channel, an aggregate institutional analysis,
or a draft checklist/request for human review.

Minimize personal data, maintain restricted evidence separately from redacted
working/public copies, and retain explicit review/hold/disposal records.
