## Execution and evidence method

This was the bounded inventory/metadata lane of a multi-agent audit. The installed `aiwg-model-efficiency-worker` wrapper was loaded first; its declared routing metadata is `model: haiku`, `model-role: efficiency`, and `model-tier: economy`. The runtime exposed the wrapper declaration but no independent model-identity attestation, so this report treats the tier as configured routing evidence, not proof that the provider honored the hint.

The `research-quality-audit` and `grade-on-ingest` skills were loaded before triage. The audit then:

1. inventoried approximately 1,893 distinct REF identifiers and 1,901 structured reference records;
2. filtered titles and records for the scoped capability domains;
3. inspected reference metadata and analysis depth, plus targeted extracted source text where available;
4. checked each shortlisted REF and distinctive title against AIWG's `agentic/`, `src/`, `docs/`, `test/`, README, and changelog;
5. removed duplicates and records with obvious existing incorporation hits; and
6. applied GRADE-on-ingest baselines: peer-reviewed work starts HIGH, preprints MODERATE, and blogs VERY LOW. The corpus's letter rating is retained separately because it reflects the local, source-specific assessment.

No exact REF/title hit was found in AIWG for any of the 50 candidates below. This is a triage signal, not proof that a concept is wholly absent: generic mechanisms such as memory, routing, and evaluation already exist and require design-level gap comparison before implementation. REF-720, REF-803, and REF-900 were excluded because direct AIWG references already exist.

**Evidence notation:** `HIGH|MOD|LOW` is the GRADE baseline; the letter is the corpus assessment; the final term is this audit's incorporation-confidence judgment. `text` means extracted full text was locally available and sampled; `PDF` means the archived source was available; `record` means the structured literature record was the primary evidence.

## Portfolio view and recommended sequencing

The highest-leverage first wave is not the largest subsystem. It is a small set of kernel evaluation and routing contracts:

1. **Skill effectiveness:** REF-1237, supported by REF-1029 and REF-1030.
2. **Difficulty/cost-aware orchestration:** REF-1637, REF-1535, REF-1536, and REF-1206.
3. **Tool discovery quality:** REF-1032, REF-1038, REF-1041, and REF-1164.
4. **Artifact reliability:** REF-714 and REF-718.
5. **Evaluation validity:** REF-1453, REF-1235, and REF-749.

The best bounded addon opportunity is an **MCP evaluation pack** combining REF-1035's production-aligned task design with REF-1036/1037 regression breadth and REF-1038's description-quality checks. The strongest new-framework hypothesis is an **autonomous research lab** grounded in REF-1050 and REF-1240, but it should remain behind explicit evidence and human-intervention gates until benchmarked against the current research mission implementation.

## Limitations and follow-up

- This lane optimized fast, auditable triage, not full-paper systematic review. A standard/deep-review worker should verify empirical designs, effect sizes, baselines, licensing, and replication status before adoption.
- Several 2026 works are recent preprints. Their high relevance does not override the MODERATE GRADE baseline.
- Exact title/REF absence is only an incorporation screen; the next pass must compare each proposal to existing AIWG behavior and open issues to avoid concept-level duplication.
- The corpus letter grades and GRADE baselines use different scales. They are intentionally shown together and must not be collapsed into one score.
- No external corpus file was modified.
