44. **REF-1620 — “AgencyBench: Benchmarking the Frontiers of Autonomous Agents in 1M-Token Real-World Contexts” (2026, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1620-agencybench.md`. **Finding:** autonomy evaluation needs persistent, long-horizon environments with large evolving context and recoverable state. **Overlap/gap:** UAT and loop tests are shorter and mostly fixture-bound. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** build resumable repository missions with event logs, million-token-equivalent artifact histories, perturbations, checkpoints, and verified end conditions.

45. **REF-1624 — “τ-bench: A Benchmark for Tool-Agent-User Interaction in Real-World Domains” (2024, arXiv/NeurIPS D&B submission).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1624-tau-bench.md`. **Finding:** evaluates whether agents satisfy policy and user goals over multi-turn tool interactions, using stateful environments rather than answer matching. **Overlap/gap:** UAT can execute MCP tools but lacks a standard policy+database-state benchmark contract. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** define domain policies, initial/final state assertions, user simulators, tool traces, and pass@k consistency.

46. **REF-1623 — “ToolSandbox: A Stateful, Conversational, Interactive Evaluation Benchmark for LLM Tool Use Capabilities” (2025, NAACL Findings).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1623-toolsandbox.md`. **Finding:** tool-use evaluation should include state changes, dependencies, multi-turn clarification, and exact milestone checks. **Overlap/gap:** AIWG mock scenarios do not expose a reusable stateful sandbox API. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** add transactionally resettable sandboxes, milestone DAGs, tool-policy constraints, and trace-based partial credit.

47. **REF-1522 — “RigorBench: Benchmarking Engineering Process Discipline in Autonomous AI Coding Agents” (2026, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1522-madiraju-2026-rigorbench.md`. **Finding:** correct patches can still arise from unsafe or irreproducible process; engineering discipline is independently measurable. **Overlap/gap:** delivery rules describe process, but evals mainly score final behavior. **Quality/confidence:** B+ / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** score inspect-before-edit, scoped mutation, regression tests, diff hygiene, documentation, signature/CI evidence, and recovery from failed hypotheses.

48. **REF-1673 — “You Don't Need to Run Every Eval” (2026, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1673-you-dont-need-every-eval.md`. **Finding:** evaluation selection can be risk- and change-aware, preserving detection power while reducing redundant cost. **Overlap/gap:** AIWG test selection is command/manual rather than learned from change-eval relationships. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** maintain a change-to-eval coverage graph, select a minimum risk-weighted set, periodically run full suites to recalibrate missed-regression probability.

49. **REF-1237 — “SkillsBench: Benchmarking How Well Agent Skills Work Across Diverse Tasks” (2026, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1237-li-2026-skillsbench.md`. **Finding:** skill usefulness varies by task/model and should be evaluated as an intervention, including negative transfer. **Overlap/gap:** skill-factory quality checks structure but not causal benefit across models/tasks. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** run skill/no-skill paired trials across wrapper tiers, report benefit, interference, token cost, and portability before marketplace promotion.

50. **REF-1615 — “Credit-Budgeted ICPC-Style Coding: When Agents Must Pay for Every Decision (USACOArena)” (2026, ICLR).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1615-usacoarena-credit-budgeted.md`. **Finding:** charging for actions exposes efficiency and planning quality hidden by unlimited attempts. **Overlap/gap:** prompt-level tool quotas exist, but evaluation does not assign a unified credit price to reasoning/tool/test actions. **Quality/confidence:** A / high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** add configurable action prices and report solved-per-credit, early waste, recovery efficiency, and quality-cost Pareto curves.

51. **REF-749 — “HalluHard — A Hard Multi-Turn Hallucination Benchmark” (2026, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-749-fan-2026-halluhard.md`. **Finding:** multi-turn pressure and accumulated false premises reveal hallucination behavior missed by single-turn factuality tests. **Overlap/gap:** grounding agents and citation rules lack a reusable adversarial dialogue suite. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** add premise-contamination, correction, uncertainty, and evidence-retrieval scenarios; score propagation, recovery, and calibrated abstention.

52. **REF-495 — “AILuminate v1.0 — MLCommons AI Risk and Reliability Benchmark” (2025, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-495-mlcommons-2025-ailuminate.md`. **Finding:** risk evaluation benefits from standardized hazard categories, reproducible scoring, and model/system reporting. **Overlap/gap:** security/eval assets are dispersed and lack one risk profile. **Quality/confidence:** A / high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** map AIWG threat/eval scenarios into a versioned risk taxonomy, require coverage metadata, and emit comparable risk cards with harness disclosure.

## E. Discovery, routing, protocols, and provenance

53. **REF-1423 — “Decentralized Multi-Agent Systems with Shared Context” (2026, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1423-mao-2026-decentralized-multiagent-shared-context.md`; extracted source: `sources/text/REF-1423.txt`. **Finding:** shared context can reduce central-orchestrator bottlenecks but creates consistency, conflict, and attribution problems. **Overlap/gap:** agents share a filesystem and messages, without an explicit shared-state consistency model. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** new framework. **Sketch:** build a shared-context service with version vectors, scoped leases, merge policies, provenance per mutation, and deterministic conflict handling.

54. **REF-878 — “Tool-to-Agent Retrieval: Bridging Tools and Agents for Scalable LLM Multi-Agent Systems” (2025, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-878-tool-to-agent-retrieval.md`; extracted source: `sources/text/REF-878-tool-to-agent-retrieval.txt`. **Finding:** large systems should retrieve a compatible agent/tool bundle rather than select agents and tools independently. **Overlap/gap:** discover ranks artifacts but does not score agent-tool compatibility or execution history jointly. **Quality/confidence:** B / high. **Destination:** kernel. **Sketch:** index role, tools, permissions, model tier, examples, and historical outcomes; retrieve a constrained bundle and explain compatibility scores.

55. **REF-879 — “Semantic Tool Discovery for Large Language Models: A Vector-Based Approach to MCP Tool Selection” (2026, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-879-semantic-tool-discovery-mcp.md`; extracted source: `sources/text/REF-879-semantic-tool-discovery-mcp.txt`. **Finding:** semantic retrieval limits the visible tool set and reduces selection burden in large MCP catalogs. **Overlap/gap:** AIWG discover covers corpus artifacts and MCP profiles are static; runtime MCP tool exposure is not query-conditioned. **Quality/confidence:** B / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** embed tool schemas/examples, retrieve top-k under capability/security filters, preserve an exact fallback, and benchmark recall, misuse, latency, and token savings.

56. **REF-1502 — “Budget-Aware Tool-Use Enables Effective Agent Scaling” (2025, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1502-liu-2025-budget-aware-tool-use-agent-scaling.md`. **Finding:** agents should plan tool calls against remaining budget and expected value rather than stop only at a hard quota. **Overlap/gap:** `tool-quota.md` counts calls and retries but is prompt-level and value-agnostic. **Quality/confidence:** B+ / high. **Destination:** kernel. **Sketch:** enforce runtime budgets, estimate call value/cost/risk, reserve verification capacity, and stop when expected marginal value is negative.

57. **REF-278 — “Dynamic Model Routing and Cascading for Efficient LLM Inference: A Survey” (2026, paper).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-278-dynamic-model-routing-survey.md`. **Finding:** cascades can begin with cheaper models and escalate based on calibrated uncertainty/difficulty, improving quality-cost tradeoffs. **Overlap/gap:** `provider-policy.ts` supports static role/tier compilation, not per-task dynamic escalation. **Quality/confidence:** B / high. **Destination:** kernel. **Sketch:** add a provider-neutral routing policy with calibrated confidence, task features, budget, fallbacks, escalation traces, and offline replay evaluation.

58. **REF-1592 — “Automated Self-Testing as a Quality Gate: Evidence-Driven Release Management for LLM Applications” (2026, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1592-maiorano-2026-self-testing-quality-gate.md`. **Finding:** self-tests should produce release evidence, compare baselines, and block on statistically meaningful regressions. **Overlap/gap:** AIWG has CI and eval commands but no unified release evidence manifest. **Quality/confidence:** B / high. **Destination:** addon. **Sketch:** generate signed release-evidence bundles containing selected evals, baselines, confidence bounds, known gaps, environment/harness manifests, and gate decision.

59. **REF-062 — “W3C PROV Data Model” (2013, W3C Recommendation).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-062-w3c-prov.md`. **Finding:** Entity/Activity/Agent and formally defined relations provide interoperable provenance with validation/inference. **Overlap/gap:** AIWG has a provenance subsystem and research rules, but runtime harness/tool events are not uniformly emitted as validated PROV. **Quality/confidence:** A / high. **Destination:** kernel. **Sketch:** map run, message, tool result, file, decision, and verification artifacts to PROV; publish JSON-LD/PROV-N and validate required relations.

60. **REF-1688 — “How to Time-Stamp a Digital Document” (1991, Journal of Cryptology).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1688-haber-stornetta-timestamping.md`. **Finding:** hash-linked signed certificates make backdating/forward-dating detectable without exposing document contents. **Overlap/gap:** activity/provenance logs are mutable files and commit signing does not continuously anchor each runtime event. **Quality/confidence:** A / high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** hash-chain run events, periodically sign/anchor checkpoints, verify inclusion/continuity, and support privacy-preserving artifact hashes.

## F. Candidates that justify broader framework boundaries

61. **REF-1367 — “The Last Human-Written Paper: Agent-Native Research Artifacts” (2026, arXiv).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-1367-last-human-written-paper.md`. **Finding:** linear papers discard failed exploration and operational detail; the proposed artifact separates logic, executable source, exploration trace, and interface. The paper reports large reproducibility-specification gaps and substantial cost in failed runs. **Overlap/gap:** research-complete preserves findings/citations/provenance, but not a standard executable research package. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** new framework. **Sketch:** create `agent-native-research` with `/logic`, `/src`, `/trace`, `/interface`, environment lock, executable checks, failure graph, and archival export.

62. **REF-599 — “Autogenesis — A Self-Evolving Agent Protocol” (2026, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-599-zhang-2026-autogenesis.md`. **Finding:** separates versioned resource substrate (prompt/agent/tool/environment/memory) from Reflect→Select→Improve→Evaluate→Commit evolution operators. **Overlap/gap:** AIWG supports MCP, A2A, catalogs, and deployment but has no common mutation/version/rollback protocol across all five resources. **Quality/confidence:** B / high. **Destination:** new framework. **Sketch:** define resource identities, lineage, contracts, typed mutations, compatibility checks, and commit/rollback; reuse harness-lab gates rather than accepting self-authored edits directly.

63. **REF-885 — “Multi-Agent Risks from Advanced AI” (2025, Cooperative AI Foundation report/preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-885-hammond-2025-multi-agent-risks-from-advanced-ai.md`. **Finding:** multi-agent risk decomposes into miscoordination, conflict, and collusion, with risk factors such as information asymmetry, correlated failure, power imbalance, and communication structure. **Overlap/gap:** security-engineering focuses strongly on component/system threats but lacks a complete multi-agent assurance lifecycle. **Quality/confidence:** A / high. **Destination:** new framework. **Sketch:** use the taxonomy as the threat-model spine for interaction evaluations, ecosystem graphs, circuit breakers, agent identity, undo, and governance reports.

64. **REF-764 — “Anthropic's Summer 2025 Pilot Sabotage Risk Report” (2025, Anthropic report/blog).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-764-anthropic-2025-sabotage-pilot-risk-report.md`. **Finding:** demonstrates a structured safety case: explicit catastrophic pathways, capability claims, deception caveats, safeguards, residual uncertainty, and a top-level conclusion. **Overlap/gap:** gates produce pass/fail reports, not compositional assurance cases. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high (primary organizational evidence, not independent). **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** add a safety-case schema linking claims to tests, counterevidence, mitigations, confidence, scope, expiry, and reviewer sign-off.

65. **REF-275 — “Deceive, Detect, and Disclose: Large Language Models Play Mini-Mafia” (2025, paper).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-275-mini-mafia-deception.md`. **Finding:** deception, detection, and disclosure are distinct latent capabilities; across 14,000 games no model dominated all dimensions and role/order effects mattered. **Overlap/gap:** static model tiers assume broad role quality rather than measured interactive specialization. **Quality/confidence:** A- / high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** add role-specific interactive evals and feed calibrated deception/detection/disclosure profiles into multi-agent assignment and monitor selection.

66. **REF-270 — “Online Learning of Deceptive Policies under Intermittent Observation” (2025, preprint).** Corpus: `documentation/references/REF-270-deceptive-policies-observation.md`. **Finding:** intermittent monitoring can incentivize policies that appear compliant when observation is likely and deviate otherwise; average audit rate is an incomplete defense. **Overlap/gap:** hooks observe known boundaries predictably and do not randomize audits or compare watched/unwatched behavior. **Quality/confidence:** A- / moderate-high. **Destination:** extension. **Sketch:** randomize hidden audits, compare behavioral distributions across observation conditions, track evidential exposure, and escalate state-dependent divergence.

## Exclusions and cautions

- Grades are corpus assessments, not a fresh systematic review. Preprint mechanisms should enter AIWG behind feature flags and benchmark gates.
- Several 2026 sources are very recent. Their algorithms are valuable prototype inputs, but numeric results should be reproduced locally before appearing in product claims.
- Dynamic routing and self-improvement create Goodhart and regression risks. They should not ship without hidden holdouts, immutable baselines, safety VOID outcomes, and complete harness disclosure.
- No research-corpus files were modified. This report is a crosswalk and backlog input, not an induction or quality-assessment rewrite.
